Why we exist

Many capable students in rural tanda communities lose access to opportunities not for lack of ability, but for lack of early identification, structured preparation, mentoring and guidance on scholarships and documents. Nagara Foundation is being set up to help close that gap, working with families, schools and local mentors.

Nagara Foundation is a proposed organisation. Programmes described here are plans unless marked otherwise.

The Class 4-to-Career pathway

  1. Discover potential
    Identify educational interests, needs and strengths.
  2. Build foundations
    Develop literacy, numeracy, reasoning and learning habits.
  3. Access opportunities
    Support suitable scholarship and entrance-exam preparation.
  4. Explore higher education
    Guidance on educational choices and progression.
  5. Prepare for careers
    Understand skills, further study and career pathways.

This is a long-term institutional ambition, not a guarantee of any exam result, scholarship or job.
