About Nagara Foundation

Vision

A future in which every child can access educational opportunities and pursue their potential regardless of geography, household income or background.

Mission

To identify educational needs and potential among students in underserved rural communities and support their learning through foundational education, mentoring, scholarship preparation, educational guidance and longer-term pathways to higher education and careers.

Name and symbol

The nagara is a traditional large drum whose beat calls a community together — a symbol of awakening (जागर).

Values

  • Talent is universal; opportunity is not.
  • Long-term commitment over one-off interventions.
  • Evidence over assumption.
  • Dignity-first: no child or parent should feel like a charity case.
  • Local roots: local mentors and teachers, with communities as partners.
  • Transparency with families and funders.

Governance

Independent, nonpartisan and focused on educational outcomes. See Transparency.
